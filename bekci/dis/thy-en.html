Why Turkish Airlines?

Combining our structural advantages with well executed growth strategy, we generate value well above the airline industry.

Global Flight Network

Ranking 1st in the world by number of countries, our network reaches more than 90% of the world's population and trade volume.

Strategic Hub

One of the largest airports in the world, Istanbul Airport sits at the crossroads of the most important global passenger and cargo corridors.

Modern Fleet

With over 500 aircraft, we efficiently utilize our modern fleet across our network and aim to increase the share of new generation aircraft from 40% to over 90% by 2033.

Aviation Ecosystem

Vertically integrated subsidiaries provide operational flexibility, cost efficiency and enhanced resilience across the value chain.

Sustainable Growth

Growing by 3x over the industry in the last two decades, we are firmly advancing towards our centennial targets.
